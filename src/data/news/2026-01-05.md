The 8th edition of the **Software Engineering Research in India (SERI)** Update Meet will be held at IIIT Hyderabad from July 16–18, 2026. SA4S research group lead **Karthik Vaidhyanathan** is serving on the organizing committee for the event.

SERI 2026 serves as a premier forum for the software engineering research community in India, bringing together faculty, researchers, and industry enthusiasts to discuss, interact, and collaborate on new ideas. The event is supported by the Software Engineering Research Center (SERC) at IIIT Hyderabad and iSOFT, the India Chapter of ACM SIGSOFT.
