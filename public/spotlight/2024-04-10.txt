[TITLE] Research at ICSA 2024
[TAG] Conference
[CONTENT] Members of SA4S presented research at the 21st IEEE International Conference on Software Architecture (ICSA 2024), held June 4-8, 2024 at IIIT Hyderabad, Hyderabad, India. ICSA is the premier IEEE venue for software architecture research and practice. Our presence spanned multiple tracks including research papers and posters, covering self-adaptive systems and architectural tactics for ML-enabled software.
